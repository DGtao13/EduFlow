# Privacy

EduFlow is designed as a local-first planner. Its schedule, tasks/homework, PRIVATE lesson information, and lesson topics/notes are stored on the device using the app’s local storage.

## Backups and exports

Backups, restores/imports, and data exports happen only after you choose the relevant action and a destination or source through Android’s document picker. Those files can contain personal school information, so store and share them carefully.

## Notifications

EduFlow requests the Android notification permission so it can show task and lesson reminders. Reminder settings are stored locally.

## Feedback

The in-app feedback action opens an email intent in an installed email client. The deliberately generated draft includes the EduFlow version, device manufacturer/model, and Android version; anything else you add to the email is your choice. Your email provider and client handle the message under their own policies.

## Scope of this statement

This policy describes the app behavior represented in this repository. Android, an email client, and any destination you choose for a backup or export may process data under their own policies.
